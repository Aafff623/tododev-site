Questions, answered briefly

What is relay?

A quiet workspace for you and your team of coding agents. You describe the outcome you want; your chief agent breaks it into todos, hands each one to the right agent, and keeps you in the loop while they work in parallel.

Which agents can it run?

The ones you already use — Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Cursor, OpenCode and more. relay assigns models and roles per task instead of locking you into one runtime.

Is the demo on this page real?

It is a faithful, clickable replica that runs entirely in your browser on mock data — the sidebar, screens, search and keyboard shortcuts all work, and nothing ever leaves your machine.

Where does my code run?

On your machine. relay orchestrates the agents and tools you already have installed; nothing is uploaded to run our side.

How much does it cost?

Free during the beta. The Pro plan — shared schedules, longer memory, team workspaces — will be announced later.
